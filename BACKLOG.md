# Backlog

Ideas for Clean Getaway that are agreed but not built yet. Items keep their numbers when they're done, so references stay valid.
Each item says what's wanted, then what the game does today and any open questions, so it can be picked up later.

*Nothing waiting right now.*

## Done

- **2. Export and import saves** (0.34.0): save codes in the safehouse, sealed with AES-GCM. Careers can also be named.
- **3. Pistol and lockpicks become stash items** (0.34.0): the pistol and the drill are owned and come home after a clean job; a lockpick is used up per lock (doors, latches and safes). The drill is carried to the vault in its bag.
- **8. Getting caught takes about 3 seconds** (0.34.0): six presses in 3 seconds break free, on every difficulty. Iron grip still wins once per job.
- **9. Fight a guard from the front** (0.34.0): the first hit rocks him for 1.5 seconds, the second knocks him out.
- **Throwing bags** (0.34.0, added on the way): range depends on what's in the bag; a bag thrown at the van is loaded.
- **1. Main menu** (0.35.0): Singleplayer, Co-op, Settings, Controls and tips. Dev mode lives in Settings.
- **4. Bag the loot yourself** (0.35.0): hold E at a pile of cash or gold to fill a duffel.
- **5. No driver: no van** (0.35.0): you leave on foot where you came in, with the bag in your hands. The driver also takes 10% of your cut now.
- **6. The driver leaves when the police come** (0.35.0): he takes the van's loot with him, and it's paid even if you're busted.
- **7. Police come in waves** (0.35.0): armed police in waves, player health. In co-op too since 0.37.0.
- **11. Co-op career** (0.39.0): the crew career. Its own save and save code, shared cash and goals, per-player levels, skills, gear, stash and kit, one driver hired by the host, saved on both devices with slot claiming on a new device.
- **10. Co-op on every map** (0.38.0): the long jobs too, with lasers, sensors, footprints, the uniform, rides and held actions per player.
- **Co-op netcode** (0.37.0, added on the way): a lossy 30 Hz channel for moving things, lag-compensated guest shots, a hook for your own TURN relays.
