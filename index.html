    Touch

    Left thumb: the joystick appears where you touch (push past the ring to sprint). Drag anywhere on the right to look. The buttons on the right change with what you can do; USE glows when there's something to use. Tap the keypad keys directly.

    • Outside without a mask you're just someone on the street. Inside a closed building, in a staff-only area or on a roof, anyone who sees you fills the detection meter, mask or not.
    • Day jobs: the public areas are open, so walk in unmasked and look around. Behind counters and staff doors is off limits, and staff and guards notice you back there.
    • When a guard spots you or finds a body, he takes 6 seconds to radio it in. Drop him before he finishes. A headshot drops a guard instantly; a body shot only wounds him, and he radios it in after 2 seconds unless you finish him.
    • The security guard watches every camera. If a camera spots you, he calls it in, so take him out. Shoot a camera while he's up and he raises the alarm. Or stand under a camera, look up and hold E to loop it for 25 seconds (do it again before it runs out to keep it looped).
    • Guards hear you. Sprinting is loud, walking is quiet up close, crouching is silent. Doors block sight, and guards open doors as they walk through.
    • Keycards open doors with a card reader. Staff leave them lying on desks, in a different place every time.
    • Windows: hold E to pick a latch, or smash the glass (F, or shoot it). Jump while running into a window sill, a desk or a crate and you pull yourself up and over.
    • Guards and cameras raise the alarm if they find a body. You can bag a body (hold E) and dump it in a bin, or leave it and hope nobody walks past.
    • Every guard you drop carries a pager. When it goes off, hold E at the body until the call is over. Dispatch only buys 3 excuses a night.
    • Civilians who see a mask, a body or a hostage pull out their phones. Look at them and press F to shout before the call goes through. Anyone on the floor gets back up after 30 seconds unless you zip-tie them. Tied hostages follow you when you press E.
    • A drill or a crane runs by itself once it's going, but it's loud: guards nearby will hear it and come and look.
