Clean Getaway

Case the place, slip in, crack the vault and get every bag to the van before anyone knows you were there. If the alarm goes off, the police arrive in 60 seconds.

  • Outside without a mask you're just someone on the street. Inside the closed bank, or on its roof, anyone who sees you fills the detection meter, mask or not.
  • Doors block sight. Guards open doors as they walk through and close them behind them.
  • The drill runs by itself once it's set up, but it's loud: guards within about 6 m will hear it and look.
  • A headshot drops a guard instantly. A body shot only wounds him, and he radios it in after 2 seconds unless you finish him.
  • The security room guard watches every camera. Shoot a camera while he's up and he raises the alarm. Take him out and the cameras go dark.
  • Guards and cameras raise the alarm if they find a body or a body bag. Bag bodies and dump them in a bin.
  • Every guard you drop carries a pager. When it goes off, hold E at the body until the call is over. Dispatch only buys 3 excuses a night.
  • Guards hear you. Sprinting is loud, walking is quiet up close, crouching is silent.
  • Day jobs: the lobby is public, so walk in unmasked and look around. Behind counters and staff doors is off-limits, and staff and guards notice you back there.
  • Civilians who see a mask, a body or a hostage pull out their phones. Look at them and press F to shout before the call goes through. Anyone on the floor gets back up after 30 seconds unless you zip-tie them.
  • Tied hostages follow you when you press E, and stay when you press it again. Guards, cameras and other civilians react to hostages they can see, so stash them out of sight.
