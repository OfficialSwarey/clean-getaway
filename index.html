It's 02:40 and the bank is closed. Three bags of cash sit in the vault. Case the place from the street through the front windows first. When you're ready, put on your mask, get inside, set the drill on the vault and carry every bag to the van in the back alley. If the alarm goes off, police arrive in 60 seconds.

  • Outside without a mask you're just someone on the street. Inside, anyone who sees you fills the detection meter, mask or not.
  • Doors block sight. Guards open doors as they walk through and close them behind them. Crouch below the window sills to stay hidden.
  • The drill runs by itself once it's set up, but it's loud: guards within about 6 m will hear it and look.
  • Once the mask is on, anyone who sees you fills the detection meter. Break line of sight and it drains.
  • A headshot drops a guard instantly. A body shot only wounds him, and he radios it in after 2 seconds unless you finish him. Shoot cameras to blind them, but only after the security room guard is down, or he raises the alarm.
  • Guards and cameras raise the alarm if they find a body or a body bag. Bag bodies and dump them in a bin.
  • Guards hear you. Sprinting and drilling are loud, walking is quiet up close, crouching is silent. Wear headphones to hear their footsteps.
  • Two ways in: the front door past the lobby, or the back door in the alley.
  • Bags slow you down. You can carry one at a time.
